About Us – Dikapei Culinary Institute

Our Story

Founded in the heart of Gaborone, Botswana, the Dikapei Culinary Institute (DCI) was established to meet the growing demand for professional culinary and hospitality training in Southern Africa. From its inception, the school has been committed to blending traditional culinary artistry with modern innovation, preparing students for both local and international careers.

Our Mission

We believe that culinary education is more than mastering recipes—it is about cultivating creativity, discipline, and a deep respect for food and culture. Our mission is to provide world‑class training that equips students with the skills, confidence, and integrity to thrive in the global hospitality industry.

Industry Connections

DCI maintains strong partnerships with hotels, restaurants, and catering companies, offering internships and job placements. Our graduates have gone on to work internationally, including in the United States and Europes, showcasing the school’s global reach.
